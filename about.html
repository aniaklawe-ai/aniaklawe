About
My story

I’m Ania, an SSI freediving instructor based in Dahab, Egypt.

I offer freediving courses and training for all levels. My background in yoga and meditation complements my teaching, combining solid technique and safety with a focus on relaxation, breath awareness, and efficient movement in the water.

With an individual approach to each student, I aim to create a safe and supportive environment where you can develop your skills, build confidence, and progress at your own pace.

For me, freediving is about more than depth or performance. It’s about learning to relax, moving with less effort, and discovering what you’re capable of — without unnecessary pressure.
