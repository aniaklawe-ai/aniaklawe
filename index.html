Why Dahab?

Why Dahab? Warm water. Year-round diving. World-famous dive sites. A relaxed atmosphere that makes learning enjoyable. From the calm Lighthouse Bay to the iconic Blue Hole, Dahab is one of the best places in the world to learn freediving.
