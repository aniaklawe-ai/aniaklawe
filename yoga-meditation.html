Yoga & Meditation

Come back to stillness.

Breath, movement and quiet attention — on the mat, and carried into the water.

Freediving asks for a calm mind and an easy breath. Yoga and meditation build both. These sessions stand on their own, and they also deepen everything you do underwater — better equalisation, longer breath-holds, and a quieter mind at depth.
