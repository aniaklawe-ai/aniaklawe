Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before your first session in the water.

Courses & Training

Do I need to know how to swim?

Yes. For Try Freediving you need to be able to swim; the certification courses (Freediver and above) require you to swim a set distance without swim or buoyancy aids — 100m for Freediver and Advanced Freediver, 400m for Performance Freediver. If you’re unsure whether you meet the requirement, just ask.

Is freediving safe?

Freediving involves risks, but proper training and safety procedures are essential to managing them. All my sessions include instructor supervision and established safety protocols, with small groups of no more than three students per instructor. Safety always comes first, and you’ll be guided throughout your time in the water.

What’s the difference between Try Freediving and the Freediver course?

Try Freediving is a one-day introduction for those who want to experience freediving without committing to a full certification course. You’ll learn the basics of breathing, relaxation, and safety, and experience freediving in the water, with no performance requirements.

The SSI Freediver course is a more comprehensive programme that leads to certification upon meeting the required standards. It develops your technique, equalisation, and safety skills, preparing you to freedive with a trained buddy within the limits of your certification.

What if I’m not confident in the water?

Let me know how you feel, and we’ll adapt the pace to your needs. We’ll focus on relaxation, breathing awareness, and gradually building your confidence in the water, without unnecessary pressure.

Are courses available in Polish?

Yes — courses, training and yoga sessions are available in both English and Polish.

Do you need a medical certificate?

A completed medical questionnaire is required for all courses. Depending on your answers, a medical certificate from a doctor may also be needed — this will be confirmed with you before your course.

Planning Your Visit

How do I get to Dahab?

The nearest airport is Sharm El Sheikh International Airport (SSH), approximately 85–90 km from Dahab. The drive takes around 1–1.5 hours, depending on traffic and checkpoints.

I recommend arranging your taxi or airport transfer in advance, either through your accommodation or with my help.

Where should I stay in Dahab?

Dahab offers accommodation for different budgets, from simple guesthouses to boutique hotels and beachfront stays. The town is relatively small, and many places are within walking distance of cafés, restaurants, and the seafront.

Airbnb and hotel booking websites are good places to start looking.

If you’re unsure where to stay, feel free to get in touch. I’m happy to recommend accommodation options based on your budget and preferences.

Is Dahab safe?

Dahab is a popular destination for travellers and freedivers, with a relaxed atmosphere and a welcoming local community. As with any destination, it’s important to stay aware of your surroundings and check the latest travel advice before your trip. I recommend checking your government’s travel guidance for Egypt before travelling.

Do I need a visa to visit Dahab?

Visa requirements depend on your nationality, how you enter Egypt, and how long you plan to stay. Some visitors travelling to Dahab for up to 15 days may qualify for a free entry permit for the Sinai Peninsula, while others need a tourist visa.

Check the latest requirements for your passport before travelling through the official Egypt e-Visa portal.

Do I need travel or freediving insurance?

I strongly recommend having both travel insurance and insurance that specifically covers freediving. Standard travel insurance may not cover freediving activities, so check that your policy covers the type of diving you plan to do.

Make sure you understand the coverage, exclusions, and any depth limits before your trip.

Bookings & Practical Information

How do I book a course or training session?

Get in touch with me to discuss your experience, goals, and preferred dates. I’ll help you choose the right course or training option and confirm availability.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes, a deposit is required to secure your booking. The amount and payment details will be confirmed when we arrange your course or training sessions.

What is your cancellation policy?

I understand that plans can change. Cancellation and refund terms depend on the type of booking and how much notice you give. I’ll explain the applicable terms before your booking is confirmed.

What happens if the weather or sea conditions are unsuitable?

Your safety always comes first. If conditions are unsuitable for freediving, we may need to postpone or cancel the session. If I cancel a course or training session for any reason, you’ll have the choice to reschedule or receive a full refund.

Can I reschedule my booking?

If your plans change, please let me know as soon as possible. I’ll do my best to find an alternative date, subject to availability and the applicable booking terms.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. Full freediving equipment is included in every course. For training sessions, equipment hire (wetsuit, mask, fins) is available for €15/day if you don’t have your own.

What should I bring on the first day?

Swimwear, a towel, sun protection, and water. If you have your own mask, fins or wetsuit bring’them as well.
