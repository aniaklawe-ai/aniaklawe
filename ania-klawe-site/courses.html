SSI Freediving Courses

Freediving is often seen as a sport of depth and breath-holding. In reality, it’s a practice of relaxation, awareness, and trust.

Whether you’re completely new to the underwater world or looking to refine your skills, each course is designed to help you become calmer, safer, and more confident in the water. Progress comes naturally when you learn to breathe well, relax deeply, and move with ease.

Every course follows internationally recognised SSI standards while combining freediving technique with breathing awareness, relaxation, and elements of yoga and meditation. The result is more than a certification — it’s an experience that often changes the way you relate to the water, to your breath, and to yourself.

Why me

Why learn with me?

Every instructor teaches the SSI standards. What makes the experience different is how those standards are taught. My coaching combines modern freediving technique with breathing awareness, relaxation, and elements of yoga and meditation. The goal isn’t simply to dive deeper — it’s to help you become calmer, move more efficiently, and enjoy every moment in the water.

Whether your goal is to complete your first certification, improve your technique, or simply feel more at ease in the sea, you’ll receive patient, personalised coaching in a supportive environment where progress happens naturally.

  • — Small groups and personalised coaching
  • — Calm, encouraging atmosphere with no unnecessary pressure
  • — Strong emphasis on safety, relaxation and efficient technique
  • — Breathing and mindfulness practices that support your diving
  • — Individual feedback tailored to your experience and goals
  • — Beautiful training locations in Dahab
  • — Courses available in English and Polish
