Training

Continue Your Freediving Journey

Already certified? Training sessions for certified freedivers who want to build confidence, refine technique, improve equalisation, increase depth, or simply spend more time in the water.

Whether you’re training towards your next certification or enjoying recreational freediving, each session is conducted in small groups with individual coaching and professional safety.

Included

In every session

  • — Instructor feedback and safety supervision
  • — Small groups — maximum 3 students per instructor
  • — Full buoy set-up
  • — Weights
  • — Video feedback

Blue Hole sessions also include taxi, permit, and National Park fees (currently €20 per person).

Not included

 

  • — Equipment hire (wetsuit, mask, fins, etc.) — available for €15/day

Group discounts are available. Don’t see a package that fits? Get in touch and we’ll figure out what works for you.

Sessions

Sample training sessions

Every session is built around your goals — these are starting points, not fixed packages.

01

Equalisation & Technique

Focused coaching on equalisation, finning and streamlining — the fundamentals that unlock effortless depth.

02

Depth Progression

Guided depth sessions with full buoy set-up, weights and safety supervision, building distance one dive at a time.

03

Blue Hole Session

Train at Dahab’s iconic Blue Hole, with taxi, permit and National Park fees included in the session.

04

Video & Feedback

Underwater video review to refine your form, paired with individual instructor feedback after every dive.

Looking for something different?

Travelling with friends? Training for a specific goal?

Want multiple sessions during your stay? We offer discounts for group bookings and customised training packages. If you don’t see exactly what you’re looking for, get in touch and we’ll create a training plan that suits your goals, schedule, and experience level.
