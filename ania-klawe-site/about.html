My story

Teaching stillness in a place built for it

I live and teach in Dahab, on Egypt’s Sinai coast — one of the freediving world’s quietest, most generous waters. My path to the sea came through breath: through yoga and meditation first, and freediving after. The two have never really been separate for me.

Every course I teach follows internationally recognised SSI standards, but the way I teach them combines modern freediving technique with breathing awareness, relaxation, and elements of yoga and meditation. The goal isn’t simply to dive deeper — it’s to help you become calmer, move more efficiently, and enjoy every moment in the water.

Progress, in my experience, comes naturally once you stop chasing it.
